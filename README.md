# TutorConnect
TutorConnect is a two-sided platform that connects students looking for home tutors with tutors looking for teaching opportunities in their nearby areas.
The idea is simple: students can discover qualified tutors near their homes, while tutors can find students, offer their teaching services, and earn money by teaching.

The platform acts as a bridge between students and tutors, making the process of finding, connecting, and hiring a home tutor easier, faster, and more transparent.


# Project description
TutorConnect is a location-based home tutoring marketplace designed for both students and tutors.

Unlike traditional methods of finding tutors through personal references, posters, or local contacts, TutorConnect provides a centralized platform where students and tutors can connect based on location, subject expertise, availability, and budget.

For students:
- Find home tutors near their location.
- Search tutors based on subjects, classes, experience, and expected fees.
- View tutor profiles, qualifications, ratings, and reviews.
- Contact tutors and request teaching sessions.
- Find tutors who match their learning requirements and budget.
For tutors:
- Create professional tutor profiles.
- Showcase qualifications, teaching experience, and subjects.
- Discover students looking for tutors nearby.
- Receive teaching requests from students.
- Set preferred fees, availability, and teaching locations.
- Build a student base and earn income through teaching.

# 🎓 TutorConnect
### Find the Right Tutor. Teach. Learn. Earn.

TutorConnect is a location-based home tutoring platform that connects students searching for nearby home tutors with tutors looking for teaching opportunities and additional income.

The platform creates a common marketplace where students can discover trusted tutors based on their location, subjects, qualifications, experience, availability, and fees. ## 🚀 Key Features

### 👨‍🎓 For Students
- **Nearby Tutor Discovery:** Find home tutors around your location.
- **Smart Search & Filters:** Search by subject, class, location, experience, and budget.
- **Tutor Profiles:** Explore tutor qualifications, expertise, experience, fees, and reviews.
- **Location-Based Matching:** Connect with tutors willing to teach in your area.
- **Teaching Requests:** Send requests to tutors based on your requirements.
- **Reviews & Ratings:** Make informed decisions using feedback from other students.
- **Direct Communication:** Connect with tutors to discuss teaching requirements.

### 👨‍🏫 For Tutors
- **Create Tutor Profile:** Showcase educational qualifications, experience, and teaching expertise.
- **Find Nearby Students:** Discover students looking for tutors in your preferred locations.
- **Manage Teaching Opportunities:** Receive and manage student requests.
- **Set Your Preferences:** Specify subjects, classes, teaching locations, availability, and expected fees.
- **Grow Your Income:** Connect with more students and build a sustainable tutoring career.
- **Build Credibility:** Collect ratings and reviews from students.

### 🔐 General Features
- Secure user authentication and authorization.
- Separate student and tutor dashboards.
- Location-based search and discovery.
- Responsive and mobile-friendly interface.
- Profile management.
- Request and booking management.

## 💡 How It Works

**For Students**

1. Register as a student.
2. Enter your location and learning requirements.
3. Discover available tutors nearby.
4. Compare profiles, experience, ratings, and fees.
5. Send a teaching request.
6. Connect with the tutor and arrange classes.

**For Tutors**

1. Register as a tutor.
2. Create your teaching profile.
3. Add subjects, qualifications, experience, and fees.
4. Set your preferred teaching locations.
5. Discover students looking for tutors.
6. Accept suitable requests and start teaching.

## 🏗️ Core Modules

1. **Authentication Module:** Registration, login, role-based access, and authorization.
2. **Student Module:** Student profiles, learning requirements, tutor discovery, and requests.
3. **Tutor Module:** Tutor profiles, qualifications, subjects, availability, and teaching preferences.
4. **Location Module:** Find and filter tutors or students based on geographical proximity.
5. **Matching Module:** Match students and tutors using subjects, class levels, location, and budget.
6. **Request Management:** Send, accept, reject, and track teaching requests.
7. **Review Module:** Ratings and feedback to establish trust and credibility.
